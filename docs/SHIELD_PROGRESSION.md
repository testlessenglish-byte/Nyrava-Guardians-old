# Shield Progression

Status: **IMPLEMENTED** gates and previews; **PROTOTYPE** visuals.

1. Basic: join once.
2. Protector: complete Password Safety, Phishing Defense, and Personal Information Safety.
3. Guardian: complete ten missions and mastery checks.
4. Defender: Level 10 plus the applied protection challenge.
5. Champion: two substantial certificates from distinct paths.
6. Elite: Level 20 plus advanced mastery and creator safety.
7. Legendary: multi-skill Guardian Capstone; XP alone cannot unlock it.

The current geometric N-mark board previews are temporary. Supplied concept PNGs were not represented as optimized game-ready 3D assets.
