# Certificate System

Status: **DIGITAL SAFETY FOUNDATIONS IMPLEMENTED**; later advanced certificates remain planned.

The first working certificate is **Digital Safety Foundations**. It is issued only after the learner completes and passes all three required foundation assessments:

1. Phishing Defense
2. Password Safety
3. Personal Information Safety

Each assessment requires a score of at least 75%. Passing grants the normal mission progress and the certificate is issued once all three requirements are satisfied. The certificate record includes completion date, curriculum version, certificate ID, covered skills, and verification status.

The Academy provides an earned-certificate gallery and a printable certificate sheet that can be printed or saved as PDF through the browser.

Future advanced certificates can add larger learning paths, mastery thresholds, capstones, and external verification. Nyrava Guardians certificates are educational credentials for the Nyrava curriculum and are not presented as government or school accreditation.
